# Financial Goals!

[Financial Goals](financial.jpg.jpg)

Five years after graduation, I hope to be financially independent and in a much better financial position. I want to have a secure career, multiple sources of income, and a significant quantity of money saved and invested.

I also want to have good credit, avoid excessive debt, and be able to afford the things I like without constantly worrying about finances. One of my goals is to leverage the abilities I obtained in school to generate extra revenue through freelance work, business, or digital products.
