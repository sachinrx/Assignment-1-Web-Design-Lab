# Lifestyle and Experiences

![Lifestyle and Great Experiences](lifestyle.jpg.jpg)

Five years after graduation, I hope to have built a lifestyle that allows me to enjoy all the work i put in. I want to travel, see new places, own a car that I enjoy driving, and have enough time to spend with the people who are important to me.

I don't want success to mean just having a nice job. I want to have enough freedom and stability to enjoy life while still chasing larger goals and more money.
